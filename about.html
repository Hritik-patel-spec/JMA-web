Welcome to Jesus and Mary Academy

Established in 1972 at Patna City, Bihar, Jesus and Mary Academy holds a place of pride in the academic world. Sponsored by a Govt. registered society (Under Societies Registration Act 21, 1860), JMA has been meeting the growing demand for value-based multipurpose English Medium co-education.

💡 The name "Jesus and Mary" symbolizes the sublime affection between Mother and Son, representing the sacred bond expected between a teacher and a student.
